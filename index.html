US Government Cyber Threat Framework

Four stages trace adversary activity from preparation through consequence; four analytical layers capture stage, objective, action, and indicator.

1Preparation

Research, planning, resource building.

2Engagement

Direct contact, phishing, exploitation.

3Presence

Privilege, persistence, concealment.

4Effect

Theft, modification, denial, disruption.

Module 08 / 11

Kali Linux Foundations and Safety

Kali is a Debian-based security distribution maintained by OffSec for penetration testing, forensics, and security research. Use it only on systems and networks you own or are explicitly authorized to test.

Standard workstation

At least 2 GB RAM and 20 GB disk for Xfce plus kali-linux-default.

Resource-intensive tooling

8 GB RAM or more and extra disk space for tools such as Burp Suite and large captures.

Installer media

amd64 Installer ISO, 8 GB USB/DVD for bare metal, imaging software, and BIOS/UEFI access.

Critical warning

A full-disk installation erases the selected disk. Back up important data first.
